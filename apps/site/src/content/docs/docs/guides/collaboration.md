## Live by default

When someone moves a card, edits a task or comments, everyone looking at the project sees it within a frame or
two. Avatars on a task show who else has it open.

Descriptions are edited together: each person's caret is labelled with their name, and edits merge as people
type. Nobody locks the description and nobody overwrites anyone. Going offline is fine: edits sync when you
reconnect.

## Comments

Type `@` to mention someone, and they are notified. React with 👍 🎉 ❤️ 😄 👀 🚀; pressing a reaction again
takes yours back. Authors can edit their comments, and admins can remove any.

## Files

Drop files onto a task's Attachments section, or pick them. Images get a thumbnail, and files can be up to 25 MB.
Downloads use short-lived signed links.

## Notifications

You are notified when someone assigns you, mentions you, or comments on your work. Notifications arrive in your
**Inbox** and as a toast if you are online.

If you are not, an **email summary** collects what is unread: daily by default, weekly, or never. Choose this in
_Account settings → Email_. The summary is sent only when there is something new.
